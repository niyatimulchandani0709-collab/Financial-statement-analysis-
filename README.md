📌 Project Overview

This project provides a structured analysis of a company's financial statements using Microsoft Excel.

The analysis transforms financial data into organized tables, calculated financial ratios, key performance indicators, and visual charts to make financial performance easier to understand.

The project is designed as a practical financial data-analysis portfolio project demonstrating Excel, financial analysis, data visualization, and business insight skills.

---

📑 Financial Analysis Modules

01. Income Statement Analysis

The Income Statement analysis evaluates the company's operating performance and profitability.

Key Areas

- Revenue
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
- Gross Profit
- Operating Expenses
- Operating Profit
- Net Profit
- Profitability trends

Objective

To understand how revenue is converted into operating profit and ultimately net profit.

---

02. Balance Sheet Analysis

The Balance Sheet analysis evaluates the company's financial position.

Key Areas

- Total Assets
- Current Assets
- Non-Current Assets
- Total Liabilities
- Current Liabilities
- Long-Term Liabilities
- Shareholders' Equity

Objective

To understand the company's asset structure, liabilities, and overall financial position.

---

03. Cash Flow Analysis

Cash Flow analysis evaluates how cash moves through the business.

Key Areas

- Operating Cash Flow
- Investing Cash Flow
- Financing Cash Flow
- Net Change in Cash
- Operating Cash Flow Trend

Objective

To understand the company's ability to generate and utilize cash through its business activities.

💵 Operating Cash Flow Trend

The Operating Cash Flow chart tracks cash generated from core business operations.

Purpose:

- Monitor operating cash generation
- Identify changes in cash flow

---

📊 Gross Profit Margin vs Net Profit Margin

This visualization compares two important profitability margins.

Gross Profit Margin → Indicates profitability after direct costs.

Net Profit Margin → Indicates profitability after operating and other expenses.

Purpose:

- Compare gross and final profitability
- Identify margin changes
- Understand the effect of expenses on final profitability

💡 Key Financial Insights

The analysis focuses on identifying meaningful patterns such as:

📈 Revenue Performance

Examines whether revenue is increasing, decreasing, or remaining relatively stable.

💰 Profitability

Evaluates changes in gross profit, operating profit, and net profit.

💵 Cash Generation

Reviews operating cash flow to understand cash generated from core operations.

🏦 Liquidity

Uses liquidity ratios to assess the company's ability to meet short-term obligations.

⚖️ Financial Leverage

Analyzes debt relative to equity to understand the company's capital structure.

📊 Margin Efficiency

Compares gross and net profit margins to understand how costs affect profitability.

📌 Financial Position

Reviews assets, liabilities, and shareholders' equity to understand the overall financial structure.

«Important: Financial insights should be interpreted from the actual underlying financial data. This project is intended for analytical and educational portfolio purposes and does not constitute investment advice.»
